Mental Health & Wellness

This page connects people with immediate support, treatment locators, peer resources, and practical next steps. It does not replace licensed medical or mental-health care.

Need help right now?

Call or text 988 to reach trained crisis support. If there is immediate danger, call local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

AI-Assisted Resource Navigation

Youth and Adult Support Pathways

AI assistance can help organize resource links, explain options in plain language, prepare questions for appointments, and guide people toward public support services. It is not a therapist, doctor, emergency service, or substitute for professional care.

Youth Resource Guide

Helps families find school, counseling, mentoring, tutoring, wellness, and community-support resources for young people.

Adult Resource Guide

Helps adults sort through care options, treatment locators, peer groups, benefits, housing links, workforce support, and next-step checklists.

Recovery Resource Guide

Organizes public recovery resources for addiction, alcoholism, family support, peer groups, and community-based help.

Follow-Up Helper

Helps prepare call scripts, appointment questions, reminder checklists, and outreach notes so people do not have to start from a blank page.

Boundary: AI can organize public information and help people prepare. It cannot diagnose, treat, prescribe, replace a licensed provider, or handle urgent situations.

Stress, Burnout, and Isolation

Start small: eat, hydrate, rest when possible, reduce immediate demands, contact one trusted person, and make one appointment or support call. Persistent or worsening symptoms deserve professional attention.
