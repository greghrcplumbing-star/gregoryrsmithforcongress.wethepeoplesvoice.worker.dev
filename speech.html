Gregory R. Smith

ILL•NOISE TO THE ECHO

★

Our founding fathers began this journey generations ago with the belief that We the People could build one nation under God. They understood that united we stand and divided we fall. If they could be here today—and if they are looking down on us now—I believe they would recognize this as a historic day: The day our nation chose to come back together.

Our nation began with thirteen colonies that stood together, declared their independence, and became the first thirteen states of a new Union.

Delaware stepped forward FIRST.

Pennsylvania held it together as the KEYSTONE.

New Jersey helped it GROW.

Georgia began to AMPLI•FY.

Connecticut wrote the CHARTER.

Massachusetts sparked the REVOLUTION.

Maryland opened the HARBOR.

South Carolina stood like the PALMETTO.

New Hampshire defended FREEDOM.

Virginia became the COMMONWEALTH.

New York made the STATE•MENT.

North Carolina gave it FLIGHT.

And Rhode Island raised the TIDE.

George Washington surrendered power so this nation would belong to the people—not to one man, one office, or one party.

Vermont remained INDEPENDENT.

Kentucky told us to RUN.

Tennessee stepped forward to VOLUNTEER.

Ohio gave it a HEARTBEAT.

Louisiana gave it RESONANCE.

Indiana set the PACE.

Mississippi gave it FLOW.

Illinois made the ILL•NOISE.

And Alabama brought the STEEL.

Abraham Lincoln held the Union together because he knew a nation divided against itself could not endure.

Maine became the ANCHOR.

Missouri said, PROVE it.

Arkansas kept it NATURAL.

Michigan supplied the DRIVE.

Florida caught the CURRENT.

Texas kept us GROUNDED.

Iowa carried the HEARTLAND.

Wisconsin moved us FORWARD.

And California placed it in the SPOT•LIGHT.

John F. Kennedy called Americans to serve because a strong nation is built by what its people are willing to give.

Minnesota followed the NORTHSTAR.

Oregon became the BRIDGE.

Kansas held the CENTER.

West Virginia carried us over the MOUNTAIN.

Nevada found the SILVER.

Nebraska built the HOMESTEAD.

Boulder became BOLD•ER.

North Dakota chose PEACE.

And South Dakota built the MONUMENT.

Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. carried a dream of justice through peace because he believed America could rise above hatred and division.

Montana faced the HORIZON.

Washington helped us EMER•GE.

Idaho stayed ROOTED.

Wyoming demanded EQUALITY.

Utah worked like a HIVE.

Oklahoma said it should have happened SOONER.

New Mexico taught us to ENCHANT.

Arizona rose like a PHOENIX.

Alaska opened the FRONTIER.

And Hawaii brought the HAMMER.

And when all 50 states stood together, they caused a ripple effect that became THE ECHO for generations to come—putting smiles back on the faces of Mount Rushmore and honoring our fallen leaders whose voices were suppressed along the journey.

One nation under God.

United we stand.

Divided we fall.

We the People become THE ECHO.

Gregory R. Smith for Congress · We The People Voice
