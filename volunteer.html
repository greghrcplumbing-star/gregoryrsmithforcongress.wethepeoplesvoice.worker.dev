Volunteer · Outreach · Sponsorship Support

Get Involved

Help with events, outreach, education resources, media, logistics, public contact research, sponsor support, venue support, and community engagement.

Campaign Support Needs

The goal is to lower campaign costs by building community support city by city.

  • Public venues or meeting rooms
  • Tables, chairs, tents, and basic event supplies
  • Printing, flyers, signage, and media help
  • Food, water, and volunteer support
  • Introductions to public sponsors and community partners
  • Public phone numbers, public emails, and official contact forms for outreach

Outreach Lanes

Community Partners

Reach out to public-facing community centers, nonprofits, churches, neighborhood groups, unions, schools, and civic organizations.

Local Sponsors

Ask businesses and organizations if they can contribute lawful support, sponsor a table, provide supplies, or share the campaign event.

Facility Support

Look for donated or reduced-cost rooms, halls, patios, parking lots, booths, tables, chairs, and event space for public campaign stops.

Public Contact Only

Use official public emails, public phone numbers, public websites, and contact forms. Do not use private personal contact information.

Website Traffic

Direct people to the campaign website so they can read the speech, join the tour, submit concerns, volunteer, and review resources.

Follow-Up

Track who was contacted, what they offered, what needs confirmation, and what must be reviewed before being announced publicly.
