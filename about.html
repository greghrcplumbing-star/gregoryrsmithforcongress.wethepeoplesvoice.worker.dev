Meet Gregory R. Smith

A working-class voice for the American people.

Gregory R. Smith is the creator of We The People Voice and a write-in candidate for the United States House of Representatives. This campaign is built around listening first, telling the truth plainly, and bringing everyday Americans back into the conversation.

This is not about political theater. It is about service, accountability, community strength, and practical work that helps families, workers, veterans, small businesses, and future generations.

Core Values

  • People before party
  • Truth before politics
  • Service before status
  • Accountability before excuses
  • Community before division
  • Transparency before backroom decisions

Mission Statement

My mission is to restore trust in public service by listening directly to the people, bringing community concerns forward, and fighting for practical solutions that serve families, workers, veterans, small businesses, and everyday Americans.

Vision Statement

The vision is an America where leadership is honest, accessible, accountable, and connected to the people it serves — a country where every community has a voice, every family has a fair chance, and government works for the people again.

My Journey

From real work to public service.

I know what it means to work with my hands, build from the ground up, solve problems under pressure, and keep moving when life gets difficult. That experience matters because the American people do not need another polished speech from someone disconnected from real life. They need someone willing to listen, show up, and fight for practical results.

I am running because too many families feel ignored. Too many workers feel squeezed. Too many communities are asking for help and getting speeches instead of action. This campaign is my commitment to bring those voices forward.

I do not work for political insiders. I do not work for party machines. I do not work for special interests. I work for the American people.

Who I Work For

I work for the people.

Families

Families deserve safe communities, affordable living, strong schools, better healthcare access, and leadership that understands everyday pressure.

Workers

Workers deserve respect, opportunity, fair wages, safer workplaces, stronger trades, and a government that values the people who keep this country moving.

Veterans

Veterans deserve better access to benefits, housing support, mental health resources, recovery programs, and real follow-through after their service.

Small Businesses

Small businesses deserve fewer unnecessary barriers, clearer rules, stronger local support, and a fair chance to grow.

Youth & Future Generations

Young people deserve stronger pathways through education, trades, financial literacy, civic involvement, and real opportunity.

Everyday Americans

This campaign belongs to the people who feel unheard, overlooked, or left out of the process. Your voice matters here.
