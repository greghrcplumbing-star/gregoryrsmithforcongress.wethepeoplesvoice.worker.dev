Community Issues Dashboard

This campaign is not one person talking over everybody. This page is built so people can submit the issues they are dealing with, the campaign can organize those concerns into clear priorities, and Gregory can bring the strongest facts to colleagues, community leaders, and decision-makers.

The mission: talk to everybody, find the best situation, move toward the fastest workable solution, and track results people can actually see.

The Fundamentals

How The Work Gets Done

1. Listen

Collect what people are actually saying from forms, events, calls, emails, and community conversations.

2. Organize

Group issues by category, urgency, location, who is affected, and what solution would create the fastest useful result.

3. Bring It Forward

Take the strongest priorities to colleagues, community leaders, public offices, nonprofits, and partners who can help move the work.
