Voter Pledge Center

This page is for supporters who want to pledge support for Gregory R. Smith as a write-in candidate for the United States House of Representatives and identify the national issues they want prioritized. This is a campaign pledge/intake page, not an official government ballot or election vote.

Security Standard

The public-facing form is structured so it can be protected before launch. The goal is one real person, one pledge record, with anti-spam and review controls before anything is counted publicly.

  • Hidden honeypot field blocks basic bots.
  • Email and phone fields support follow-up verification.
  • IP/rate-limit protection should be turned on in Cloudflare.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA should be added before public promotion.
  • No public tally should display until submissions are reviewed.
Important: For election compliance, this records supporter pledges only. It should never be labeled as an official vote.
